## 1. Project Title / Headline

**📊 PeoplePulse: HR Analytics & Attrition Insights Dashboard**
An interactive Power BI dashboard designed to analyze employee attrition, performance, job satisfaction, demographics, and departmental earnings—helping organizations make data-driven HR decisions.

---

## 2. Short Description / Purpose

The **PeoplePulse HR Analytics Dashboard** is a comprehensive Power BI report built to analyze employee attrition patterns, workforce demographics, job satisfaction levels, and department-wise earnings. It enables HR teams and business leaders to identify attrition risks, understand workforce behavior, and improve retention strategies using data-driven insights.

---

## 3. Tech Stack

The dashboard was built using the following tools and technologies:

* 📊 **Power BI Desktop** – Primary data visualization and reporting tool
* 🔄 **Power Query** – Data cleaning, transformation, and feature engineering
* 🧠 **DAX (Data Analysis Expressions)** – Custom measures for attrition rate, KPIs, averages, and percentages
* 🧩 **Data Modeling** – Structured HR dataset with calculated fields and relationships
* 📁 **File Format** – `.pbix` for development and `.png` for dashboard previews

---

## 4. Data Source

**Source:** HR Analytics Dataset (HR Employee Records)

The dataset contains employee-level information including:

* Demographics (Age, Gender, Education)
* Job details (Department, Job Role, Performance Rating)
* Compensation (Monthly Income)
* Employment metrics (Years at Company, Years Since Promotion)
* Attrition status

The data was cleaned, enriched, and categorized (age bands, education fields) to support advanced analysis and interactive filtering.

---

## 5. Features / Highlights

### 🔹 Business Problem

Employee attrition directly impacts productivity, hiring costs, and organizational stability.
HR teams often struggle to answer questions like:

* Which departments face the highest attrition?
* Does age, gender, or education influence attrition?
* How does job satisfaction relate to employee turnover?
* Which departments generate the highest earnings?

Without an interactive analytics tool, these insights remain hidden in raw HR data.

---

### 🎯 Goal of the Dashboard

To deliver a centralized HR analytics solution that:

* Monitors overall employee attrition and workforce health
* Identifies high-risk age groups, departments, and roles
* Analyzes job satisfaction and performance trends
* Supports HR decision-making for retention, compensation, and workforce planning

---

### 📌 Walkthrough of Key Visuals

#### **🔹 Overall Workforce KPIs (Top Section)**

* **Total Employees:** 1,470
* **Attrition Count:** 237
* **Attrition Rate:** 16.12%
* **Active Employees:** 1,233
* **Average Employee Age:** 36.92

Provides an instant snapshot of workforce health.

---

#### **🔹 Performance Analysis Page**

* Median Monthly Rate vs Monthly Income by Education
* Average Years at Company vs Performance Rating by Years Since Last Promotion
  Helps identify how education and promotion cycles influence income and performance.

---

#### **🔹 Attrition Rate by Department**

* Pie chart comparing attrition across **HR, Sales, and R&D**
  Highlights departments with the highest employee turnover.

---

#### **🔹 Job Satisfaction Analysis**

* Tabular breakdown of job satisfaction ratings (1–4) across job roles
* Attrition count by education field (Life Sciences, Medical, Marketing, etc.)
  Reveals dissatisfaction trends linked to specific roles and education backgrounds.

---

#### **🔹 Age-wise & Gender-wise Attrition**

* Donut charts for age groups:

  * Under 25
  * 25–34
  * 35–44
  * 45–54
  * Above 55
* Gender-based attrition comparison within each age group
  Helps HR teams identify vulnerable age and gender segments.

---

#### **🔹 Department-wise Earnings**

* Department-wise contribution to total monthly income
* Average monthly income by age group and gender
  Supports compensation benchmarking and pay equity analysis.

---

### 📈 Business Impact & Insights

* **Attrition Risk Identification:** Quickly identify departments and age groups with high attrition
* **Retention Strategy Support:** Align HR policies with satisfaction and performance insights
* **Compensation Planning:** Understand income distribution across departments and age bands
* **Workforce Optimization:** Enable leadership to make informed staffing and promotion decisions
* **HR Reporting Automation:** Replace manual HR reporting with dynamic dashboards
